Give your kids a daily TV time quota that you control from your phone. When Child mode is on, the app counts how long your TV is switched on. When the quota is used up, the TV is switched off and stays locked until you give extra time or turn Child mode off. It works with any TV that is a device in Homey, and directly with LG webOS TVs. A dashboard widget and Flow cards let you manage it from Homey. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by LG Electronics.

The app asks for full access to Homey so that it can list your TVs and switch the one you choose off and on. It reads and changes nothing else.
