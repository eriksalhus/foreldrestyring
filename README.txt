Give your kids a daily TV time quota that you control from your phone. When Child mode is on, the app counts how long your TV is switched on. When the quota is used up, the TV is switched off and stays locked until you give extra time or turn Child mode off.

The app works with any TV that is already a device in Homey, or talks directly to an LG webOS TV over your home network. A widget for your Homey dashboard shows the time left and lets both parents turn Child mode on or off, give extra time and see how much time has been used today and this week. Anyone can still switch the TV on with the remote, but while it is locked it is switched off again within seconds. Child mode can be switched off automatically when a new day starts, and switched on automatically at a time you choose. Both are set on the settings page, where you also choose when the day starts (05:00 by default).

Works with any TV that has been added to Homey (the app switches it off and on through that device), and with LG webOS TVs by IP address. Messages on the TV screen (minutes left, time is up) work with LG webOS TVs, directly by IP address or, for a TV in Homey, through a supported LG app after a one-time link; they can be switched off. Tested directly on an LG G2 (webOS 22); other TVs have not been tested.

The app asks for full access to Homey so that it can list your TVs and switch the one you choose off and on. It reads and changes nothing else.
